## Project overview

T-OCS V7.0 REVISION-2 is a rear-seat occupant-control system concept being advanced through engineering definition and evidence closure. NAIF Automotive Safety Lab is an independent automotive occupant-safety research and engineering initiative under SILVER TRUCKERS LLC.

The near-term aim is to scope a disciplined next step with qualified technical or industrial partners. Public material is deliberately limited to L0; this repository is not a substitute for controlled engineering disclosure or evidence review.

## Partner pathways

We welcome initial discussions with qualified organisations whose capabilities are relevant to one or more of these areas:

- Automotive seat-system engineering and engineering-definition review
- Physical test-article and fixture design/fabrication
- Dynamic or sled-test planning, instrumentation and measurement
- Manufacturing, automotive suppliers, and OEM/Tier-1 evaluation
- Technical co-development, applied research, licensing, or milestone-based strategic support

An initial contact should identify the organisation's relevant capability, the proposed contribution, and a practical next step. No confidential dimensions, geometry, or mechanism details are needed for the first message.

## Development status and evidence boundary

Current focus: **engineering definition / engineering completion and evidence closure**.

Intended future pathway:

1. Engineering definition
2. Physical test article and fixture
3. Dynamic / sled testing
4. Measurement and correlation
5. Validation

The later stages are planned or partner-dependent unless separate evidence establishes completion. This repository does **not** claim validated safety performance, certification, regulatory compliance, production readiness, completed dynamic/sled-test results, OEM endorsement, customer traction, or a particular patent status.
