# P0 Functional Article Minimum Requirements

## Minimum physical article

A reduced **dual-side carriage rig** containing both bilateral longitudinal load paths, a carriage, a controlled energy-management path, a mechanically separate seatback rotation-control path, a state-transition lock, rebound-control, and a development-only restraint interface. It may be reduced in packaging relative to a finished vehicle seat, but it must preserve the stated mechanical relationships.

## Can prove

It can demonstrate fit, guided translation, independent rotation-control motion, state transition, reverse-motion control, and instrumented developmental force/displacement behavior when actual evidence exists.

## Cannot prove

It cannot prove occupant safety, vehicle compatibility, crash performance, certification, homologation, production equivalence, or FE validity.
