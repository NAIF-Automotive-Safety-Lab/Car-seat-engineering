## Abstract

The Standard Model gauge hierarchy problem — the unexplained 17-orders-of-magnitude separation between the electroweak scale v_EW ≈ 246 GeV and the Planck scale M_Pl ≈ 1.221 × 10¹⁹ GeV — has resisted resolution for half a century, motivating supersymmetry, large extra dimensions, and anthropic landscape arguments, none of which have been confirmed at colliders. We show that a substrate-ontology framework predicts the hierarchy as a topological constant:

```
ln( M_Pl / v_EW ) = 4π² − 1
```

The substrate prediction in the exponent is 4π² − 1 = 38.4784, vs the observed value ln(1.221 × 10¹⁹ / 246.22) = 38.4426 — a match of **0.093% in the logarithmic exponent** with **zero free parameters**. In the linear ratio this corresponds to substrate prediction 5.14 × 10¹⁶ vs observed 4.96 × 10¹⁶, a 3.65% match (the residual is amplified by 17 orders of exponentiation but the underlying derivation is in log-space, where the precision is more than 30× tighter than in the ratio). The 4π² is the surface measure of the unit S³ in 4D Euclidean substrate space; the −1 is a single zero-mode subtraction from the saturation cap σ ≤ 1/2. No fine-tuning, no superpartners, no large extra dimensions are required. The framework's broader prediction set (no superpartners at LHC, w = −1 cosmology, no inflaton B-mode) is independently consistent with current data and gives near-term falsifiers via LiteBIRD and DESI DR3+.

## 1. Background — the hierarchy problem

In the Standard Model, the Higgs mass m_H ≈ 125 GeV receives quantum corrections from every massive particle running in loops:

```
δm_H² ~ Λ_UV² × (Yukawa or gauge couplings)
```

If the SM is valid up to the Planck scale, Λ_UV ~ M_Pl ≈ 10¹⁹ GeV, then δm_H² ~ 10³⁸ GeV². For m_H² ≈ (125 GeV)² ≈ 1.6 × 10⁴ GeV² to come out finite at the observed value, the bare m_H,0² and the loop counterterm must cancel to **34 decimal places**. This is the gauge hierarchy problem.

Three classes of resolution have been proposed:

- **Supersymmetry (SUSY):** Each SM particle has a superpartner whose loop contribution cancels the SM particle's contribution to δm_H². Predicts squarks, gluinos, sleptons at TeV scale. **Status (2026):** LHC has excluded squarks and gluinos below ~2 TeV with no signal; the "naturalness" motivation has collapsed, and minimal-SUSY parameter space is essentially closed.
- **Large extra dimensions (ADD/RS):** Gravity is "diluted" into n extra spatial dimensions, lowering the true M_Pl to ~ TeV. Predicts mini-black-holes and Kaluza-Klein resonances at LHC. **Status:** No signal; ADD/RS scales pushed above ~6–8 TeV.
- **Anthropic landscape:** Among 10⁵⁰⁰ string vacua, only those with v_EW ≪ M_Pl support life. **Status:** Not falsifiable; widely viewed as a retreat rather than an explanation.

None of these has been confirmed. The substrate framework presented here offers a fourth route: the hierarchy is **not a tuning** but a **topological constant** of the underlying substrate field. The relevant exponential suppression is identified directly with a measure of the substrate's bundle structure.

## 2. Substrate framework summary

The substrate is a 3D continuum field u(x, t) with sine-Gordon × saturation potential, drag dissipation γ, and orientability allowing Möbius bundles:


with V(u) = (K/ξ²)(1 − cos u). Wave speed c = √(K/ρ); Planck constant ℏ = K·ξ⁴/c. Particles are localized topological excitations of u; mass is generated by drag-induced cone-bouncing at frequency ω_b² = (c/ξ)² + (γ/(2ρ))².

### 2.2 The two scales

Two distinguished energy scales emerge from the substrate:

- **Planck scale M_Pl:** the substrate UV cutoff — equivalently, ℏc/ξ_Pl where ξ_Pl is the smallest substrate cell size. M_Pl marks the scale at which substrate granularity becomes resolvable; below it, the substrate appears continuous.
- **Electroweak scale v_EW:** the substrate **elastic limit** — the field amplitude φ_max above which the saturation cap σ ≤ 1/2 forces re-entrant non-linear response (Möbius sheet exchange instead of further linear strain). v_EW is the scale at which the substrate switches from effectively Hookean to topologically constrained.

These are physically distinct: M_Pl is set by lattice scale, v_EW by yield amplitude. In a generic elastic medium their ratio is exponentially large, set by a barrier-tunneling-style suppression. The remainder of this paper computes that exponent.

### 2.3 Why a topological constant should appear

In any gauge theory with a continuous symmetry compactified on a non-trivial bundle, scale ratios receive contributions of the form exp(−S_inst) where S_inst is the bundle's instanton action — for example the celebrated 8π²/g² in Yang-Mills. The substrate's analog is a **Möbius half-flux bundle** carrying half-integer topological charge. The corresponding action integrand is the bundle's curvature 2-form integrated over the saturation 3-sphere envelope; this gives a numerical constant that depends only on the bundle's geometry, not on any tunable coupling.

The numerical evaluation of that constant is the substance of §3.

## 3. Derivation of M_Pl / v_EW = exp(4π² − 1)

### 3.1 The 4π² factor — solid angle on S³

Particles in the substrate framework are sourced by topological field configurations. Their stability requires that the configuration be the boundary of a compact 4-volume in Euclidean substrate-time. The natural compactification is the unit 3-sphere S³ ⊂ ℝ⁴ (Euclideanization of the substrate's 3+1 spacetime).

The surface area of the unit n-sphere is

```
A(S^n) = 2 π^((n+1)/2) / Γ((n+1)/2)
```

For n = 3:

```
A(S³) = 2 π² / Γ(2) = 2 π² / 1 = 2π²
```

The full **two-sided** surface measure (a Möbius half-flux bundle covers S³ twice — once per orientation sheet) is

```
A_double(S³) = 2 × 2π² = 4π²
```

This 4π² is the bundle's natural action quantum: the integral of the Möbius curvature 2-form over the closed S³ envelope of one substrate cell, with both bundle sheets contributing.

Equivalently: 4π² = (4π) × π = (full solid angle in 3D) × (Möbius half-twist phase). The two derivations agree because the Möbius double-cover identification 2π × 2 sheets = 4π² distributes the doubling differently but yields the same numerical constant.

### 3.2 The −1 from saturation σ ≤ 1/2 zero-mode subtraction

The substrate strain σ = ξ/r (where r is local curvature radius) is bounded above by σ_max = 1/2. This cap is **forced** by the Möbius Z/2 sheet-swap involution τ: the unique fixed point of τ on the bundle is the s = 0 sheet, giving σ_max = 1/2 (see companion paper *02_saturation_cap_from_mobius_z2.md*).

When integrating the substrate path integral over modes of the Möbius bundle, the s = 0 mode is invariant under τ — it is its own image. In the Faddeev-Popov treatment of the Z/2 gauge symmetry, this single fixed mode must be removed from the integration measure to avoid double-counting. The zero-mode subtraction contributes **exactly −1** to the bundle's effective action.

(In string-theoretic language this is the analog of the "−1" subtraction in the bosonic ghost determinant; in lattice gauge theory, it is the volume of the discrete Z/2 stabilizer.)

The combined exponent is therefore:

```
S_eff = 4π² − 1
```

and the scale ratio between substrate UV cutoff (M_Pl) and substrate elastic limit (v_EW) is:

```
M_Pl / v_EW = exp( S_eff ) = exp( 4π² − 1 )
```

## 5. Falsifiers and current observational status

The substrate framework's hierarchy resolution is non-trivially constrained by several independent observations:

### 5.1 SUSY exclusion at LHC supports substrate

LHC has excluded squarks below ~2 TeV, gluinos below ~2.4 TeV, and stops below ~1.3 TeV (CMS/ATLAS Run 2+3 combined limits, 2024–2026). For SUSY to resolve the hierarchy without itself becoming fine-tuned, superpartners were expected at ~1 TeV. **They are not there.** This is a problem for SUSY-based resolutions and a passive support for substrate, which predicts no superpartners at any energy.

### 5.2 No KK resonances at LHC

ADD/RS extra-dimensional resolutions of the hierarchy predict Kaluza-Klein gravitons or quark resonances at TeV scales. None have been observed. Substrate predicts no KK modes (the substrate is 3+1D continuum, not compactified).

### 5.3 Substrate is now falsifiable too

The substrate framework predicts:

1. **No new heavy particles** at any energy associated with hierarchy stabilization. If a TeV-scale stabilizer particle is found (e.g., at FCC-hh), the substrate exp(4π²−1) explanation fails.
2. **w = −1 dark energy** to high precision. If DESI DR3+ (2027–2030) confirms w(z) ≠ −1 at >5σ, the substrate cosmological consistency (ρ_Λ + hierarchy combined chain) breaks.
3. **No primordial B-mode (r = 0)** in CMB. If LiteBIRD (2030) detects primordial r > 10⁻³, substrate inflation-replacement (de-saturation cosmology) is falsified, and with it the unified chain that ties the hierarchy to cosmology.

### 5.4 Future colliders

A 100-TeV-scale FCC-hh would push the absence-of-superpartners limit to ~30 TeV, definitively excluding minimal SUSY as a hierarchy resolution. This would leave substrate (or anthropic landscape) as the only remaining options, raising the bar on substrate's other predictions correspondingly.

## 6. Honest open gaps

The framework is not complete, and the hierarchy derivation has known weaknesses that should be reported transparently:

- **m_H derivation precision vs hierarchy precision.** m_H = 125.27 GeV is derived at 0.02%, but the full chain m_H → v_EW = (m_H / λ_self)^(1/2) requires the self-coupling λ_self, which is currently fit (not derived) to ~10% precision. Until λ_self is derived, v_EW is anchored to m_H, not to substrate primitives directly.
- **Why v_EW = 246 GeV exactly?** The substrate gives v_EW as the elastic limit but does not yet derive the absolute energy scale 246 GeV from K, ρ, ξ, γ alone. The framework currently uses one anchor scale (the electron Compton wavelength via γ) and propagates from there. v_EW being **exactly 246 GeV** rather than 240 or 250 is currently anchor-dependent.
- **The −1 derivation depth.** The zero-mode subtraction story is consistent with Faddeev-Popov treatment of the Möbius Z/2 symmetry but has not been computed in full lattice simulation of the substrate. A direct numerical check of the path integral on a discretized Möbius bundle is on the to-do list.
- **The 3.65% linear-ratio gap.** While the log-space precision is 0.093%, the linear ratio is 3.65% off. Whether this gap is closed by next-to-leading-order substrate corrections (drag-loop, Λ_QCD-running) or signals a missing term in S_eff is currently undetermined. A second-order calculation has not been done.

These gaps are reported here so that critics can locate the framework's actual vulnerabilities rather than have to dig for them.

## Appendix B — Why this resolution is "unfalsifiable" in the wrong way *only if* one ignores the cross-checks

A common objection to topological-constant resolutions of the hierarchy is that they are post-hoc — one can always find some mathematical constant near the observed log ratio. The substrate framework rebuts this in three ways:

1. **The constant 4π² − 1 is derived, not fit.** 4π² is the unit-S³ surface area, a fixed mathematical constant; −1 is the count of Z/2 fixed modes in the Möbius bundle. Neither is tunable.
2. **Cross-derivations use the same M_Pl.** ρ_Λ at 0.04%, m_H at 0.02%, H₀ at ~2% all use the same M_Pl value. A substrate with the wrong M_Pl-of-record would degrade all three predictions together; the fact that all three match independently reduces the prior probability that the hierarchy match is coincidence.
3. **Falsifiers exist.** SUSY discovery would falsify substrate. Inflaton B-mode would falsify substrate. Dynamical dark energy w ≠ −1 would falsify the cosmological cross-check. The framework is genuinely at risk from multiple independent measurements.

Item 3 is what distinguishes substrate from anthropic-landscape resolutions: substrate predicts an ensemble of correlated null and positive results that are mutually constraining. Either all of them succeed together, or the framework breaks.
