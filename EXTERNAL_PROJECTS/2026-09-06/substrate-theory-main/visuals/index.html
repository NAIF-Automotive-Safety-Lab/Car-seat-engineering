K_4 geometry

The complete graph on 4 vertices is the smallest closed K_4 cell of the substrate. Its faces, edges and vertex-pairings encode the irreducible topological data shared by every defect-bearing region.

Topological defects

Stable matter is a defect of the underlying medium. Kinks (1D), vortices (2D), monopoles (3D point), and skyrmions (3D textured) are the four classes the substrate admits, with kink–antikink scattering setting the elastic-channel kinematics.

Field evolution

Real-time evolution of the substrate field on a 2D lattice plus the 1D phonon dispersion that connects long-wavelength sound to ballistic short-wavelength bouncing.

Saturation

Strain density is bounded above. The saturation ceiling σ ≤ ½ replaces black-hole singularities with a finite cone, drives the dark-matter cube cell at q=3, and selects which configurations the medium can host.

Möbius

Half-flux topology gives spin-½ for free: a 2π rotation maps to the antipodal sheet, 4π closes back. The Möbius cycle is the framework's only required UV input.

Cosmology

Universe-wide saturation replaces the singularity with a long bleed-off era. De-saturation crossings produce the CMB phase imprint; the saturation horizon tilts the substrate cone, sourcing dark energy.

Mass tower

Cone-bouncing frequency sets mass. The lepton/quark generation tower follows from successive Möbius excitations; the nuclear chart and nucleon stacking show how this scales up to baryons and nuclei. EM radiation patterns close the QED side.

Bound states

Eigenmodes of the substrate-derived Yukawa potential, in 3D and as mode spectra. These match light hadron and atomic ground states without inheriting the Coulomb formula.

Lagrangian terms

Term-by-term contribution map of the encompassing Lagrangian (§18.45 / §18.84). Visualizes how the substrate kinetic, gradient, potential, fermion, gauge and 45° cone constraints partition observables.
