# Physical Evidence Protocol

A physical measurement record requires sensor ID, calibration reference, sampling frequency, units, timestamp, test ID, and raw-data SHA-256. The current verified physical measurement count is zero. Missing vehicle pulse, occupant/ATD, mass properties, friction, stiffness, damping, absorber, material, and boundary-condition data remain BLOCKED with acquisition plans.

Calculated or simulated results may support engineering analysis, but they cannot be relabeled as physical measurements.
