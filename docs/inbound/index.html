Project overview

A focused route from engineering definition to physical evidence.

The immediate objective is to define the right next engineering and test work packages with the appropriate partners. Every material claim must match an available artifact, calculation, model, inspection or test result.

What we are developing

  • A rear-seat occupant-control system concept.
  • Engineering definition and evidence closure.
  • Partner-defined planning for a representative test article and test path.

What is not established here

  • Validated safety performance or certification.
  • Completed dynamic or sled-test results.
  • Production readiness, regulatory compliance or OEM endorsement.

Partner pathways
